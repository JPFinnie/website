§ 01 — About

A product leader for regulated, data-driven platforms — turning ambiguous AI and platform opportunities into deliverable product work.

I’m a Senior Product Manager / Product Owner based in Toronto, working at the intersection of wealth technology, AI-enabled product strategy, and regulated digital platforms.

At CIBC Investor’s Edge, I focus on modernising digital investing experiences across client journeys, segmentation, personalisation, platform strategy, and AI-assisted product operations. My work sits between business strategy and execution — sizing opportunities, shaping product narratives, evaluating vendors, building prototypes, and helping teams translate complex ideas into practical, governed delivery.

“In regulated finance and health, legibility isn’t a constraint — it’s the product.”

Before CIBC, I led IoT and intelligent-systems product work in regulated medical technology, owning digital respiratory health products across mobile apps, connected devices, and clinician workflows. Earlier, I worked in fintech and trading product roles in London — brokerage, mobile trading, onboarding, and KYC.
