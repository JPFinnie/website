A product perspective

Context should belong to the organisation.

I think businesses should build organisationally owned and managed context systems: shared knowledge for employees, and a permissioned view of each client relationship. People and their AI tools should be able to work from context that the business takes responsibility for keeping useful.

Shared knowledge for employees

Connect decisions, research, policies and ways of working to their sources. Give that knowledge clear owners, review points and a record of what changed. Employees should be able to reuse what the organisation has learned, with AI helping maintain the connections.

Context for each client

Bring relevant communications, preferences, commitments and service history into a permissioned timeline. That could include emails, messages and call summaries where capture is appropriate and authorised, helping teams provide continuity across a relationship.

Facts first. Estimates labelled.

Keep observed events and source-backed facts separate from AI summaries and modelled estimates such as client lifetime value (CLV). Make assumptions and freshness visible, so an estimate can inform planning without being mistaken for a fact about a person.

Managed as a product

Ownership includes access rules, consent where required, a clear purpose for what is captured, and retention and correction processes. Shared context needs those controls alongside the knowledge format itself.

Ideas informing this approach

Andrej Karpathy’s LLM Wiki describes an AI-maintained, interlinked markdown knowledge base built from source material, with workflows for adding knowledge, querying it and checking its health.

Google Cloud’s Open Knowledge Format v0.2 adds optional signals for provenance, verification, freshness, lifecycle and checking computations. These signals help consumers assess context; they do not enforce access permissions.

The employee and client applications above are my proposed direction, rather than claims of a deployed system or recommendations attributed to these sources.
