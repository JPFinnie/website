[ 01 ] — About

An AI-native product leader — prototype-first, agents in the loop, and one question held constant: what are we building, and why?

I’m a Senior Product Manager in Toronto, working on AI-native product and agentic engineering. I build deep context systems — structured knowledge that agents can genuinely reason over — for my team, and as a personal LLM wiki.

At CIBC Investor’s Edge, I lead product for self-directed investing and am changing how the team ships: working prototypes in place of heavyweight specs, agent-assisted discovery, and design systems wired into agentically built prototypes — with pipelines that carry the work through to production.

When building is cheap, taste and judgement are the moat. Knowing what to build — and why — is the hard part.

The product conviction: investing at an incumbent bank shouldn’t feel generic. It should feel like the institution genuinely works for you — timely insight, and nudges in the right direction where they’re appropriate.

I.

Taste at speed

When anyone can build anything, judgement is the moat. Ship fast — and know which of a thousand possible paths deserves to exist.

II.

Ask why, relentlessly

Cheap building makes the what and the why the hard part. Conviction and discovery come before code.

III.

Prototype-first

Agent loops, skills files, and working prototypes over specs and decks. Build the thing, then decide.
