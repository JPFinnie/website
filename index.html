James FinnieProduct thinker. Hands-on builder.

I’m most useful where the problem is complicated, the path isn’t obvious, and a team needs something real to react to.

At CIBC Investor’s Edge, I lead product for self-directed investing. I use working prototypes to make strategy tangible, bring agents into discovery, and help teams test direction earlier.

Before that: connected medical devices, trading platforms, payments, and FX. Different industries; the same work of connecting people, technology, and commercial reality.

AI makes building faster. The advantage is choosing the right problem, setting a clear quality bar, and knowing why the product should exist.

The experience behind the approach ↘
01 — Judgement

Taste at speed.

02 — Curiosity

Ask why. Then why again.

Start with the person, the constraint, and the change that matters. Make the problem clear before solving it.

03 — Practice

Make it tangible.
