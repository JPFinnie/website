§ 01 — About

An AI-native product leader — building prototype-first, with agents in the loop, and a relentless question: what are we building, and why?

I’m a Senior Product Manager based in Toronto, focused on AI-native product and agentic engineering. I build deep context systems — for work and for personal use — in the spirit of a personal LLM wiki: structured knowledge that agents can actually reason over.

At CIBC Investor’s Edge, I’m moving my product discipline toward prototype-first delivery: agent loops, skills files, and working prototypes in place of heavyweight specs. I’m shifting the team from long requirement documents to PR-FAQs — one- and six-pagers — wiring design systems into agentically built prototypes, with pipelines that carry the work through to production.

When building is cheap, taste and judgement are the moat. Knowing what to build — and why — is the hard part.

On the product itself: incumbent banks feel too generic. I want investing that feels like the institution actually works for you — timely insight and nudges that point you in the right direction, where it’s appropriate to.

I.

Taste at speed

When anyone can build anything, judgement is the moat. Ship fast — but know which of a thousand generated paths deserves to exist.

II.

Ask why, relentlessly

Cheap building makes the what and the why the hard part. Conviction and discovery come before code.

III.

Prototype-first

Agent loops, skills files, and working prototypes over specs and decks. Build the thing, then decide.
