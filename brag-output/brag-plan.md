# Brag Plan: james-finnie.com — "Signal, found."

## What is this app?
A personal portfolio/resume for James Finnie — Senior Product Manager, AI & Agentic
Product, Toronto — staged as a dark fintech terminal where a particle field of noise
literally structures itself into order as you scroll. The site *is* the pitch: taste,
signal, and judgement rendered as UI.

## The angle
The site's own thesis is the video's spine: **"When building is cheap, taste and
judgement are the moat."** Open in noise — an unstructured particle field. As the
claim lands, the field structures itself (the site's signature scroll mechanic,
STRUCTURE 000% → 100%) and the signal that emerges *is James* — name, role, focus,
track record — delivered in the site's exact terminal design language (mono `[ 01 ]`
eyebrows, corner ticks, hairline lattice, datastrip). A title sequence for a person:
order out of chaos, because that's the job description.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed near-black. The particle field drifts — unstructured, webbed, restless —
split by a thin divider with the site's own labels: `STRUCTURED` / `UNSTRUCTURED`.
A terminal line types: **"When building is cheap,"** — keyboard ticks, low music
fade-in. The next line is the reason to keep watching.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The field structures itself.** Particles snap from chaotic drift into the exact
  lattice, weave lines turn orthogonal, the divider fades, and the site's HUD counter
  ticks `STRUCTURE 000% → 100%`. This is the page's narrative engine, recreated.
- **The name reveal, exactly as the hero renders it.** "James" solid ink + "Finnie"
  stroke-outlined, word-mask rise, corner ticks drawing in, eyebrow
  `[ SENIOR PRODUCT MANAGER — AI & AGENTIC PRODUCT · TORONTO ]`.
- **Focus cards as system modules.** Three fcards arrive one-by-one on the beat grid
  with their real index tags: `01 SYS/CTX Deep context systems`,
  `02 SYS/AGENT Agentic engineering`, `03 SYS/SHIP Prototype → production`.
- **The datastrip.** The hero's real career-data strip pops cell-by-cell:
  `PRODUCT CRAFT 05 YRS · ORGS SHIPPED 04 · NOW CIBC INVESTOR'S EDGE · FOCUS AI-NATIVE ▲`.

## Outro / punchline
The contact section's two words, full scale: **"Let's build."** ("build." in signal
blue, exactly like the site's `em` treatment). Below: `james-finnie.com` and a ghost
`GET IN TOUCH →` button. The field settles to a calm ordered lattice behind; music
fades under the hold.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only (it's a resume). But the page has a signature *interaction*
that substitutes for a flow: **scrolling structures the field** (chaos → lattice,
`STRUCTURE` HUD counting to 100%, divider dissolving). The video simulates that
scroll-journey as its connective tissue — the field is one continuous backdrop that
transitions from noise (hook) to order (outro), just as the real page does top to
bottom.

## Tone
- Preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: "a quiet, premium terminal title-sequence — a signal found in
  the noise; the site introduces its owner the way a trading desk boots up"
- Interpretation: dramatic reveals with polished restraint — big type, long settles,
  slow-crossfade scene handoffs over one continuous background; no gag beats, no
  clutter; confidence over volume. Motion is fast-in / long-hold.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — a steady, clean bed that swells under the reveal and
  fades under the outro hold
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (bundled; "steady
  and clean", the polished/cinematic pick), start 0.0s, volume 0.35
- Music treatment: soft fade-in over first ~0.8s; full bed through the reveal and
  highlights; fade out over the final ~2s so the outro holds in near-silence
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read
  (`assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`,
  109.96 BPM). StrongCue targets: **8.74s** (name reveal), **17.47s** (datastrip
  arrival), **22.93s** (outro "Let's build." land). Beat-grid windows: focus cards on
  every-other-beat from **13.11 → 14.20 → 15.29s**; datastrip cells on consecutive
  beats **17.47 / 18.02 / 18.56 / 19.10s**.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — music RMS may breathe the field's glow/line
  brightness and the hero vignette; no waveform/equalizer visuals, no text pulsing.
- SFX posture: sparse-to-moderate, motion-matched, professional restraint
  (cinematic: 2-3 big accents + typing ticks + card arrivals)
- Audio-coupled moments: typed hook line (randomized keypress ticks); name reveal
  (one deep bell); focus cards (card-place per arrival); datastrip cells (soft
  drops/clicks per beat); outro land (soft heavy impact, rings over the fade)
- Restraint rule: never stack more than one accent per beat; no SFX during the long
  outro hold after the land; keep all SFX under the "one sonic palette" rule —
  bells/soft impacts + UI ticks only.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Noise — 3.6s (0.0 → 3.6)
Full-bleed `#050608`. The chaos field drifts (right-biased web of dots + faint
connection lines), thin white divider at center, site labels `STRUCTURED` (left) /
`UNSTRUCTURED` (right) in mono caps. Top-left mono eyebrow: `[ 00 ] — SIGNAL / NOISE`.
Center, a terminal line **types**: `When building is cheap,` — cursor block blinking.
Sequential/interaction: yes — hook line types out character-by-character (~1.1s),
then holds settled ≥1.6s.
Audio intent: low, expectant; music fades in under fingertips.
Audio-coupled idea: randomized `keyboard/keypress-*` ticks per character.
Music: bed starts 0.0s at 0.35 with 0.8s fade-in.
Transition mood: soft (text crossfades; field persists) → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The claim — 3.8s (3.6 → 7.4)
Second line lands beneath the first, larger, display type:
**taste and judgement are the moat.** The field begins to react — particles nearest
the text start snapping to grid; `STRUCTURE 000%` HUD fades in bottom-left and starts
ticking. Divider begins to dim.
Sequential/interaction: none (single line, fast-in then hold ≥2.4s).
Audio intent: the thesis gets weight; bed continues, no accent — restraint.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the line carry).
Transition mood: dramatic (field accelerates to order) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Signal / the name — 5.2s (7.4 → 12.6)
The field fully structures — chaos sweeps into the lattice left-to-right, weave lines
turn orthogonal, divider gone, `STRUCTURE` HUD races to `100%`. Corner ticks draw in
around a centered frame. At **8.74s (beat-locked)** the name rises with the site's
word-mask move: **James** (solid ink) **Finnie** (stroke-outline). Above it, mono
eyebrow: `[ SENIOR PRODUCT MANAGER — AI & AGENTIC PRODUCT · TORONTO ]`. At ~10.4s a
dim lede settles under: `AI-native products & deep context systems — prototype-first,
agents in the loop.`
Sequential/interaction: yes — eyebrow first, name words rise one-by-one (masked),
lede after; HUD counter ticks continuously.
Audio intent: the arrival — one deep resonant accent as the name lands.
Audio-coupled idea: `impact/impactBell_heavy` on the name (beat-locked 8.74s); HUD
tick could carry a faint chips-stack tick if not busy.
Music: bed full.
Transition mood: clean (name block yields; field stays ordered) → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Focus modules — 4.8s (12.6 → 17.4)
Section header style: eyebrow `[ 02 ] — FOCUS AREAS`. Three fcards arrive one-by-one
on the every-other-beat grid (**13.11 / 14.20 / 15.29s**), each in the site's card
anatomy (mono index + tag top row, display title):
`01 SYS/CTX — Deep context systems` · `02 SYS/AGENT — Agentic engineering` ·
`03 SYS/SHIP — Prototype → production`. Full set holds ≥1.9s after the last arrival.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards, one per every-other beat, each with a card
sound; hold the full set.
Audio intent: rhythm section — the system assembling itself.
Audio-coupled idea: `casino/card-place-*` or `interface/drop_*` per card arrival
(beat-grid).
Music: bed full.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The datastrip — 3.4s (17.4 → 20.8)
The hero's datastrip, recreated: four bordered cells pop on consecutive beats
(**17.47 / 18.02 / 18.56 / 19.10s** — non-text-heavy, values are 1-3 word labels):
`PRODUCT CRAFT → 05 YRS` · `ORGS SHIPPED → 04` · `NOW → CIBC INVESTOR'S EDGE` ·
`FOCUS → AI-NATIVE ▲` (▲ in up-green). The `05` and `04` tick up. Full strip holds
≥1.7s. First cell arrival is the **17.47s strong-cue lock**.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 cells on consecutive beats + count-up values.
Audio intent: precision — small confident confirmations.
Audio-coupled idea: soft `interface/click`/`drop` per cell; counters silent.
Music: bed full, last full-energy passage.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Let's build. — 3.7s (20.8 → 24.5)
The ordered field dims to a calm backdrop. **"Let's build."** slams in at contact-title
scale, `build.` in `#5E8BFF` — beat-locked to **22.93s** for the settle accent…
(entrance begins ~21.0s, the accent lands as it settles). Under it: mono
`JAMES-FINNIE.COM` and a ghost-button `GET IN TOUCH →`. Music fades 22.5 → 24.5;
final soft impact rings past the fade. Hold to black.
Sequential/interaction: yes — title, then url + button one beat later.
Audio intent: resolution — one warm final accent, then near-silence.
Audio-coupled idea: `impact/impactSoft_medium` or `impactBell_heavy_003` at the
22.93s lock; nothing after.
Music: fades out under the hold.
Transition mood: hold to end.
